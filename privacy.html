Privacy policy

Open Board, effective 29 October 2026

Open Board is a chess app. It does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few things in your device's own storage so it can work the way you left it: your chosen computer level, an unfinished game, which puzzles you solved, and an anonymous random player code. This information does not leave your device unless you play online, as described below. Clearing the app's data removes it.

Online games

If you play a friend online, the app sends the following to our game server so both phones stay in sync: the 5-character game code, the moves played, the time the game was created or updated, and your anonymous random player code. The player code is not linked to your name or any other account. The game server is a Google Firebase Realtime Database. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms and privacy policy.

Anyone who has your game code can open that game and see the moves. Do not share the code with people you do not want to play.

Game records are kept only as long as needed to run games and may be deleted at any time.

Fonts

The app loads its text fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives the usual information any website receives, such as your IP address and device type.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No sale of data, and no sharing of data except with Google as the server provider described above.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It collects no personal information at all.

Deleting your data

Local data: clear the app's storage in your phone settings, or uninstall the app. Online game data: email us the game code and we will delete that game.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
